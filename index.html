PLANNING TOOL

Deadline Pile-Up Planner

Add your due dates and your busy hours. See which weeks get crowded and what to work on first.

Step 1. Your time

New to this tool? A short how-to video shows how to use each step.

Watch the how-to video (opens in a new tab)

When several due dates land in the same week, it is easy to feel buried. This tool adds up the hours you enter and shows which weeks look crowded, so you can plan before they arrive.

You enter every number yourself. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. The tool does not predict grades.

Weeks are counted in blocks of 7 days starting on this date. Today is filled in. You can change it.

Hours that are already taken

Enter the hours in a typical week. A rough number is fine. Leave a box blank if it does not apply.

The 70 is only a starting guess of about 10 hours a day. Change it to fit your life.

Be realistic, and leave time to rest. You can set different hours for particular weeks in the next step.

Step 2. Your tasks

A task is anything with a due date: a paper, an exam to study for, a quiz, a lab, a shift swap you need to arrange. Add each one, then guess how many hours of work it needs. Use your syllabus for the due dates.

The sizes are rough guesses. If you know how long something takes you, type the hours in the "Or type the hours" box. That box wins over the size.

Weeks when your hours are different (optional)

For a week with extra work shifts, a trip, or a day off, enter any date in that week and the study hours you will have that week.

Step 3. Your plan

You can also print with Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac).

This tool is for planning. It adds up the hours you enter. It does not predict grades, know your instructors' rules, or replace talking with an instructor, counselor, or advisor. Every number in the plan comes from you.
